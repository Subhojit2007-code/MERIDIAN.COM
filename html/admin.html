Product variants & filters

Variants are the options shoppers choose on a product page — colour, size, capacity and so on. Define the option groups here, then tick which choices each product offers under the Products tab. A group ticked as a storefront filter also appears above the shop's catalogue, where shoppers narrow the product list by the choices their products offer. Variants group cart lines and appear on the order, but they don't change the charged price: checkout re-prices from the server catalogue by product ID.
